OneNightStand Child Safety Standards

OneNightStand is a dating app intended exclusively for adults aged 18 and older. Children are not permitted to use the service.

Zero Tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation

OneNightStand prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including any content that sexually exploits or endangers minors. Such content and behavior are strictly prohibited on our platform.

Reporting Concerns

Users can report profiles or behavior they believe violate our safety rules using the reporting features available in the app. Users can also block other accounts.

Action on Violations

We will review reports of suspected child exploitation and take appropriate action, which may include removing content, suspending or terminating accounts, and reporting suspected child sexual abuse material to the appropriate authorities when required by law.

Prevention and Cooperation

We prohibit attempts to use OneNightStand to groom, exploit, solicit, or otherwise endanger minors. We will cooperate with appropriate authorities and child-safety organizations as required by applicable law.

Contact

For child-safety concerns, contact our designated child-safety team at support.onenightstand@gmail.com.
